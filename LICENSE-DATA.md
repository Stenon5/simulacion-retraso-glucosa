Attribution is required. CC BY 4.0 was chosen over a public domain dedication
because every result here is conditional on the assumptions in `docs/spec.md`.
Attribution keeps a number attached to the conditions it was produced under,
which matters more for a simulation than for a measurement.

## Citing a number from this repository

Cite the repository through [`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff) and include the
scenario and the seed. A number from here without its scenario is not
meaningful, because the result depends on the noise level and on the
separation between the two depths.
